Core System Capabilities

Explore the tools provided by the digital logbook ecosystem to streamline industrial attachments.

Daily Milestone Logging

Enables attachees to record daily operational records, skills mastered, and challenges faced during their 14-week attachment cycle.

✅

Supervisor Endorsements

Allows industry field supervisors to review, append custom training feedback, sign off, and digitally validate entries weekly.

🎓

Lecturer Assessment Hub

Provides university assessors with real-time remote visibility to track student progress, schedule assessment visits, and submit final marks.

📊

Automated Reports

Compiles logs into downloadable, cleanly formatted summaries alongside visual attachment progress analytics.
